# Registered expectations — four-route convergence paper
# Written BEFORE the ladder quantities were computed. Outcomes recorded after.

E1. Ritz additivity of circulation.
    For measured NIST hydrogen levels, circulation is additive along a chain:
    rho(n1->n3) == rho(n1->n2) + rho(n2->n3) to NIST precision.
    PREDICT: holds to <1e-6 relative.
    RISK: this is Ritz combination, so it may hold trivially. Report if so.

E2. Forbidden transitions are inert rungs.
    Transitions violating dl=+-1 or ds=0 should carry power 0 and contribute
    zero circulation.
    PREDICT: rho contribution exactly 0 for the forbidden set.

E3. The 21 cm line is the smallest non-inert rung in the H atlas.
    It is a ds rung, forbidden by the dipole selection rule, yet observed.
    PREDICT: its power is the minimum over all measured H transitions, by
    several orders of magnitude, and it is nonzero.

E4. H2O vibrational cycle: uniformity separates it from a hypothetical
    symmetric-top cycle.
    PREDICT: u < 1 for H2O (nu1, nu2, nu3 are not equal), and the value is
    computed from HITRAN rather than assigned. This closes ladder-paper L1
    for this corpus.

E5. Circulation per rung separates electronic from vibrational modalities.
    By analogy with the aromatic/saturated separation (margin +0.293).
    PREDICT: a positive margin exists.
    RISK: the aromatic result needed TWO invariants; a single-invariant
    separation may fail here exactly as it failed for cyclohexane. If it
    fails, report the failure, do not redefine the quantity.

E6. Four-route convergence.
    Established, instrument, ladder, and catalogue routes agree on every
    line to within the precision each defines.
    PREDICT: agreement. A disagreement singles out a route, which is the
    whole point of having four.
